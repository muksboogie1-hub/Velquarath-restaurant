Data Protection & Visitor Privacy

Privacy Policy

Important Notice & Owner Details Required

This Privacy Policy accurately describes the technical operation of the Velquarath Cafe website and is structured with reference to Zimbabwe’s Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] (regulated by POTRAZ as the Data Protection Authority) as well as international standards. Because legal compliance depends on the restaurant’s internal business operations, this notice does not constitute formal legal advice or a guarantee of statutory certification. Before commercial launch, the restaurant owner must supply:

  • Registered legal entity name and company registration number in Zimbabwe
  • Physical street address in Harare, Zimbabwe
  • Designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) or privacy contact email and phone number

1. Personal Data We Collect

We practice strict data minimization. When you browse Velquarath Cafe, we do not require you to create an account or submit personal data. If you choose to use our Reservation Request or Contact Enquiry forms, we ask only for your name, phone/WhatsApp number, optional email, and booking preferences.

2. How Your Data Is Processed (Static Website Operation)

This website is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. Reservation and contact forms validate your input in your browser and format a summary so you can copy it or send it directly via WhatsApp. No personal data is silently transmitted to third-party servers, marketing databases, or external analytics platforms.

3. Third-Party Services & Technical Audit

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or third-party behavioral advertising scripts. All food and venue photographs are hosted locally in the repository’s assets/images/ folder. Web fonts (Fraunces, Plus Jakarta Sans, and JetBrains Mono) are loaded from Google Fonts without setting tracking cookies.
