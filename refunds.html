Bookings, Takeaway, Delivery & Catering Terms

Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Table Reservations & Cancellations

Standard table reservations at Velquarath Cafe do not require an online deposit. If your plans change or your party size adjusts, please notify us as early as possible so we can release the table for other guests. We hold reserved tables for 20 minutes past the scheduled reservation time before releasing them to walk-in guests.

2. Dine-In, Takeaway & Delivery Orders

We take pride in preparing our flame-grilled meats, traditional Zimbabwean stews, burgers, and drinks fresh to order. If an item in your dine-in, takeaway, or delivery order is incorrect, missing, or does not meet our quality standards, please inform your server immediately or contact us within 2 hours of collection/delivery for a replacement dish or refund via your original payment method.

3. Private Dining Room & Event Catering Deposits

For large group bookings in our Private Dining Room or off-site event catering where a pre-arranged deposit is agreed directly with management, cancellations made at least 72 hours prior to the event are eligible for a refund of any deposit paid, less any non-recoverable custom procurement costs agreed in writing.
