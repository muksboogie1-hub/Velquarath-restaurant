Website Use, Menu Availability & House Rules

Terms & Conditions

1. Menu Items, Seasonal Availability & Pricing

Our menu features both signature grill items and traditional Zimbabwean dishes (including fresh Kariba bream, road runner, game curries, and seasonal starters such as madora and mopane worms). Where prices are displayed on this website, they reflect the figures supplied by Velquarath Cafe. For items without a listed online price, current pricing is displayed on our in-house menus and chalkboards at the restaurant.

2. Allergens & Dietary Information

Several traditional Zimbabwean dishes on our menu — including Roasted Nzungu, Chibage & Nzungu, Rice, Beans & Nuts, and stews enriched with groundnut butter (dovi) — contain peanuts / groundnuts. Please inform your server of any food allergies or dietary requirements before ordering.

3. Alcohol Service & Responsible Enjoyment

In accordance with Zimbabwean liquor licensing laws, alcoholic beverages — including our signature cocktails and lagers — are served strictly to persons aged 18 years and older.
