Physical Venue & Digital WCAG 2.1 AA Commitment

Accessibility Statement

1. Physical Restaurant Accessibility at Velquarath Cafe

We are committed to ensuring that every guest can dine, relax, and enjoy live music and sport comfortably at Velquarath Cafe. Our physical venue provides:

  • ✓ Wheelchair-accessible step-free entrance
  • ✓ Wheelchair-accessible dining tables & patio seating
  • ✓ Wheelchair-accessible restroom / toilet facilities
  • ✓ Wheelchair-accessible car park & free street parking
  • ✓ High chairs and kids’ menu for families
  • ✓ Table service and staff assistance on request

2. Digital Website Accessibility Features

We have designed this static HTML website to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines, including visible gold keyboard focus rings (:focus-visible), "Skip to main content" links, high-contrast typography, semantic HTML landmarks, descriptive image alt text, and automatic respect for prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
