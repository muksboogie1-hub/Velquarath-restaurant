02. Heritage & Heart

Traditional Zimbabwean Mains, Sadza & Starters

From slow-braised Oxtail & Beans, free-range Road Runner, and whole Kariba Bream with Soup to Impala Curry, Rabbit (Tsuro), Madora, and Derere Rine Matemba — served with steaming White or Brown Sadza or Rice, Beans & Nuts.

  • Oxtail & Beans

    Slow-braised tender oxtail simmered with sugar beans in a rich, savory gravy.

  • Road Runner

    Authentic free-range country chicken slow-simmered in a traditional tomato and onion broth.

  • Kariba Bream with Soup

    Whole fresh Kariba bream, flame-grilled or pan-crisped, served with aromatic soup.

  • Smoked Pork Bones & Impala Curry

    Meaty smoked pork bones, beef bones & chicken livers, rabbit masala, and grilled guinea fowl.
